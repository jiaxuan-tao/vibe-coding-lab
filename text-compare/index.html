Text Compare

Compare two texts and see exactly what changed. The comparison runs in your browser, and this site has no ads.

Privacy

  • Text you paste and files you open (Word, Excel, PDF, images) are read in your browser. Comparing never sends them anywhere.
  • History and saves stay in this browser. You can turn history off above, under Options.
  • Copy link puts the text inside the link itself, in the part after the #, which is never sent to a server.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Tips

  • Save a comparison with Ctrl S and find it later in the Library, along with your recent history.
  • Drop two files at once to compare them straight away. Word, Excel, PDF and image files work too.
  • Blocks that moved are shown in violet with a tag that jumps to where they went.
  • The Data tab compares JSON and CSV by structure, so reordered keys or rows don't count as changes.
  • Export a report to send someone, or print it as a PDF redline.
  • Both sides are editable. Use the arrows between them to copy a block across.
  • Options can ignore case, whitespace and blank lines.
